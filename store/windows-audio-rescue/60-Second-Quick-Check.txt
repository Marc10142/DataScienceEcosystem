MARC OS WINDOWS AUDIO RESCUE — 60-SECOND CHECK

1. Settings > System > Sound
   - Correct Output selected?
   - Correct Input/microphone present and selected?

2. Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone
   - Microphone access ON
   - Let apps access your microphone ON
   - Let desktop apps access your microphone ON

3. Win + R > mmsys.cpl > Recording
   - Right-click blank area
   - Show Disabled Devices
   - Show Disconnected Devices
   - Enable the correct microphone if shown

4. Device Manager
   - Audio inputs and outputs
   - Sound, video and game controllers
   - Action > Scan for hardware changes

5. If speakers work but microphone is missing:
   - Focus on privacy settings, disabled recording devices, OEM audio/chipset drivers,
     and device re-detection.

6. Use the included Diagnose launcher to generate a report.
   Use Safe Repair to restart Windows audio services and rescan hardware.

Never download drivers from random driver-updater websites.
